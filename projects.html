Purchase Propensity Model

Platform: Databricks + Unity Catalog · Binary Classification · Synthetic Data

End-to-end MLOps pipeline that predicts the probability of a customer purchasing within the next 30 days. Built on Databricks with automated hyperparameter search and batch scoring at scale.

Approach

  • Leakage-controlled feature engineering with null imputation
  • Hyperopt TPE search over learning rate, depth, estimators
  • Fitted Spark pipeline persisted to Unity Catalog Volume

Marketing A/B Test: Ad Exposure & Conversion

Dataset: Kaggle Marketing A/B Testing · Two-Proportion Z-Test · 588K Users

Causal analysis of whether exposure to an ad campaign (vs. a public service announcement) increases purchase conversion. Combines a two-proportion hypothesis test with practical-significance framing, dose-response analysis, and a stratified confound check to separate the ad effect from exposure-level confounding.

Approach

  • Two-proportion z-test for ad vs. psa conversion rates
  • 95% confidence interval and Cohen's h for effect size
  • Business-impact framing via incremental conversions

Use cases

Campaign evaluation Quantify the true conversion lift from an ad campaign vs. PSA
Budget allocation Focus spend on segments with high exposure where lift is largest
Experiment design Flag confounds (e.g. exposure dose) before running a clean RCT
